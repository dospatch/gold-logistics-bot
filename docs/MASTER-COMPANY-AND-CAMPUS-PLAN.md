# BC Logistics LLC — Master Company & Campus Plan

## Approved company scale
- Fleet: 300+ company-owned and operated trucks.
- Workforce planning capacity: 600+ employees across all divisions and shifts. This is a design capacity, not a claim that 600 people are currently employed.
- Distribution center: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- Customer base: wholesale customers only (business-to-business). Do not market direct-to-consumer or individual retail services.

## Business model
BC Logistics LLC is a regional transportation, warehousing, and wholesale distribution company. Its primary transportation model is a company fleet with employed drivers. Approved outside motor carriers may register as freight partners and use designated carrier facilities; they are not automatically employees or company-fleet drivers. The company handles inbound freight deliveries and outbound wholesale shipments.

## Campus master program
- Corporate headquarters: three floors, central atrium with two angled wings, reception, executive offices, HR, finance, meeting rooms, and a 24-hour-capable dispatch/operations center.
- Main regional distribution center: 20 loading docks, wholesale receiving and shipping, cross-dock, pallet storage, automated and manual material handling, barcode/RFID tracking, and dedicated refrigerated/freezer storage.
- Transportation yard sized and phased for 300+ tractors and corresponding trailers; final simultaneous parking count, trailer ratio, and phasing must be dimensioned in the site plan.
- Eight truck maintenance bays, dedicated inspection area, parts warehouse, truck wash, and on-site company diesel fueling.
- Separate company-fleet parking, registered-carrier staging/parking, trailer drop yard, inbound/outbound truck scales, gatehouse, check-in kiosks, and appointment control.
- Employee/visitor parking and pedestrian-safe routes separated from heavy truck circulation.
- Driver lounge, restrooms, showers, lockers, break area, training/onboarding space, and driver check-in.
- Generator and backup power, electrical/IT rooms, refrigeration backup, fire protection, cameras, controlled access, stormwater, waste/recycling, used-oil/battery containment, and emergency access.
- Security, carrier registration, freight inspection/claims, warehouse control, and facilities support spaces.

## 24/7 operating model
- Distribution receiving, shipping, dispatch coverage, yard control, security, and critical maintenance support operate around the clock.
- HR, finance, procurement, and executive administration may use scheduled business hours with on-call escalation.
- Staffing plans must model three shifts or an equivalent rotating schedule; exact shift times and staffing per shift remain to be approved.
- Plan staffing and parking for 600+ total workforce capacity without assuming all employees are on site simultaneously.

## Wholesale freight workflow
1. Wholesale customer account and credit/contract approval.
2. Shipment order and appointment creation.
3. Inbound or outbound classification, cargo details, pallet/weight data, temperature requirements, and documents.
4. Gate check-in, carrier/company verification, scale, and dock assignment.
5. Receiving, inspection, put-away, cross-dock, picking, staging, or loading.
6. Dispatch assignment and shipment tracking.
7. Proof of delivery, inventory reconciliation, invoicing, and claims handling.

## Digital operating systems
- Employee directory, department assignments, applications, onboarding, training, attendance, and staff records.
- Company fleet register, tractor/trailer assignment, inspection, maintenance, fuel, and utilization.
- Wholesale customer accounts, shipment/load board, appointments, dock schedule, inventory, carrier registration, proof of delivery, billing, and claims.
- Management reporting for fleet utilization, on-time service, warehouse throughput, dock utilization, safety, maintenance availability, inventory accuracy, and customer service.

## Planning safeguards
- 300+ trucks and 600+ employees are capacity targets, not current operating counts.
- Wholesale-only customer policy must be reflected in website copy, customer intake, and operating procedures.
- Outside carriers remain registered business partners, separate from employees and the company fleet.
- Do not invent dock assignments, shift times, parking counts, staffing ratios, or building dimensions that have not been approved.
- Preserve existing Blender work, Discord history, channels, and website functionality. No paid assets, services, subscriptions, or upgrades without explicit approval.
- Blender construction remains subject to separate explicit authorization; this plan does not authorize construction or modification of the 3D campus.
